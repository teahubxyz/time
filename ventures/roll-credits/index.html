How it works

  1. Type a title, then one person per line as "Name, role".
  2. Pick a style and a speed; the preview scrolls live.
  3. Download a 15-second WebM or GIF. It is made in this page and goes nowhere else.

Why this exists

People thank a team in a group chat, and it feels like nothing. A film ending feels like something. Roll Credits rides the wave of two repos trending on GitHub this week, both full of people who care how a thing ends. It does not use their code.

Honest limits

  • Exports are 15 seconds at the speed you chose. A longer roll is cut off; the page shows the roll length so you can raise the speed.
  • The GIF is small (320×180, 10 frames a second, a fixed 256-color palette). The WebM is 640×360.
  • WebM is recorded in real time with MediaRecorder, so it needs a browser that records WebM and a visible tab. Otherwise use the GIF.
  • No music or sound, no logos, no custom colors or fonts: three built-in styles only.
  • Nothing is saved. Reload the page and your text is gone, so keep your list somewhere.
