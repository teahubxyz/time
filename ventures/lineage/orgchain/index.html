Orgchain

Found a holding, then birth its orgs and agents, award belts, record decisions, make items, buy and sell items and orgs, publish keyrings, charter legal forms, send test BTC, graduate and close. Each act is an inscription whose parent is the node that authorizes it, paid for with the actor's own gas, and each beat is a block whose 50 test BTC the holdings mine. Lineage gives the structure; the chain adds time.
