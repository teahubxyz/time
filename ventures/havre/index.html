          Every character needs a harbor
          
    
          Characters live inside apps, and apps come and go. Havre keeps a character as a file you own, gives it a place to talk, and writes down where it came from.
          
    
          The name is a small salute. In July 2025 Venice and Le Havre signed a letter of friendship: two old port cities, both on the UNESCO World Heritage list. Havre means harbor. Our characters make the crossing.
          
    
          Havre talks to Venice's API with your own key. It is an independent project, not part of Venice and not endorsed by it.
          
  
          
  

          


          
  
          How it works
          
  
            
    
          • Keep
            Every character is a card file: Havre JSON, or a character card (V2 or V3, JSON or PNG) that SillyTavern, RisuAI and other card apps can read. It lives in your browser until you export it. Nothing is uploaded.
            • 
    
          • Talk
            Add your Venice key and talk for real; without one, characters answer from their own examples. Replies arrive as short texts, and a character can follow a flow, like Taskmaster's assess, plan, execute.
            • 
    
          • Remix with credit
            Love a character you didn't make? Havre reads it and writes down only the ideas. A second pass that never sees the original writes someone new. Havre checks the new words against the old and keeps the credit in the card's lineage.
            •

          The fine print
          
  
          
    
          Your characters, your files
          Characters and conversations are saved in this browser. Export them to keep them anywhere else.
          
    
          Your key goes only to Venice
          The key stays in this browser and is sent only to api.venice.ai: the page's security policy blocks every other address. Conversations run on your Venice account, under Venice's terms.
          
    
          Bringing over someone else's character
          Names and broad ideas aren't protected by copyright; a character's written expression can be. So a Venice character comes in as a link with credit, not a copy, and Remix writes someone new from ideas alone. When in doubt, ask the creator. This isn't legal advice.
          
    
          Not affiliated
          Havre is independent. It is not part of Venice.ai and isn't endorsed by Venice; it uses Venice's public API, with your key.
          
    
          Safe for work
          Havre only lists Venice characters that aren't marked adult.
