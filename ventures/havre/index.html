            HOW IT WORKS

            1. Paste your Venice API key. It stays in this browser, and the page can reach api.venice.ai and nothing else.
            2. Pick a character: one of Venice's, a card file you import, or one you write. Venice's characters say hello first.
            3. Talk. Replies come as a few short texts. Without a key, the characters you write answer from their own examples.
            4. Keep them. Export a character as a file, or remix them into someone new; the credit stays in the card.

            FOR AGENTS

            Copy this into any agent. It reads the skill, writes the character, and hands back the files. Change the second line to who you want.

            
    
             files: skill.md / card.schema.json / card.js / venice.js / llms.txt

            FINE PRINT
            
  
            
    
            Characters and conversations are saved in this browser. Export them to keep them anywhere else.
            
    
            Your key stays in this browser and goes only to api.venice.ai; the page's security policy blocks every other address. Conversations run on your Venice account, under Venice's terms.
            
    
            Safe for work only: Havre lists only Venice characters that aren't marked adult.
            
    
            Someone else's character comes in as a link with credit, not a copy, and Remix writes someone new from ideas alone. Names and broad ideas aren't protected by copyright; a character's written expression can be. When in doubt, ask the creator. This isn't legal advice.
            
    
            Havre is independent. It isn't part of Venice.ai and isn't endorsed by Venice.
            
  
            

            


            
  
            THE NAME
            
  
            
    
            Havre means harbor. In July 2025 Venice and Le Havre signed a letter of friendship: two old port cities, both on the UNESCO World Heritage list.
