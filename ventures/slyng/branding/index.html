SLYNG/Brand

The Y is a slingshot.

The SLYNG logo is the name in Unbounded ExtraBold with the Y doing the slinging: a volt stone on the vertex, and the band drawn up the inside of the arms to the tips. Here are the files, how to use them, and every draft it took to get here.

Get the filesSee every draft

The logo

Two versions, one drawing. White letters on ink, or ink letters on bone or white. On a light ground the bands and the stone get an ink outline, because volt alone disappears against it.

The Y
Unbounded's own Y, untouched. It is the slingshot.
The stone
A volt circle centred on the vertex of the fork, 16.4% of the letter height across.
The bands
5.6% of the letter height wide, parallel to the inside edges of the arms, from the stone to the tips, cut flush with the tops of the letters.
The gap
A hairline, 2.2% of the letter height, between each band and its arm and around the stone: the ground itself on ink, an ink outline on light grounds.
Spacing
The letters keep Unbounded's own widths, with L and Y set 40 units (of 1,000) closer. Keep clear space of at least half the letter height on every side.
Size
At least 200 pixels wide on screens and 35 mm in print. Smaller, and the hairline gap fills in: use the icon.
  • Use the files. Don't retype the name, even in Unbounded: the bands and the stone aren't in the font.
  • Keep the band and the stone volt. Don't change the colours, add effects, or stretch it.
  • White letters go on ink; ink letters go on bone or white. Not on photos or busy grounds.

Colour

Four colours, used about in these proportions. Volt is only ever the sling: the band, the stone, and the ground of the reversed icon.

  • Ink#111014RGB 17 16 20
  • Bone#F1F0ECRGB 241 240 236
  • White#FFFFFFRGB 255 255 255
  • Volt#D9FF3DRGB 217 255 61

White on ink reads at 19:1, and volt on ink at 16.5:1. Volt on white or bone is barely there (1.2:1 and 1.0:1), which is why the light logo outlines it in ink.

Type

Unbounded ExtraBold

The logo is drawn from Unbounded at weight 800, a wide, rounded sans by NaN, free from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Its Y has straight arms that meet at a sharp vertex, which is what lets the bands run along them and the stone sit on the point.

Slyng Condensed and Slyng Text

Pull back for more

The site sets its headings in Slyng Condensed and everything you read in Slyng Text, cut from TeX Gyre Heros Condensed and TeX Gyre Adventor.

Motion

When the logo moves, it fires. A three-second loop that starts and ends in the logo pose, so it can stop after any loop and leave the logo standing.

  1. Hold0 to 0.5 sThe logo, still. In the last moments the stone trembles.
  2. Release0.5 to 0.6 sThe band snaps up, straight across the tips.
  3. Flight0.6 to 0.7 sThe stone flies out of the top, getting smaller.
  4. Ring out0.6 to 1.8 sThe empty band rings around straight and settles.
  5. Reload1.8 to 2.3 sA new stone drops in, and the band catches it in a shallow V.
  6. Draw back2.3 to 2.8 sThe pouch slides down the arms until the stone sits on the vertex.
  7. Hold2.8 to 3 sAnd it loops.

How it got here

Every draft, in the order it was made, over six rounds. Each round ends with what changed next; the stone marks what was picked.

1

Round 1: Eight ways in

The brief was the name with its Y as a slingshot. Eight concepts, each with its own typeface and colours.

Concept 1Loaded

Lexend ExtraBold

The Y is a slingshot with a red band, and the stone sits in the pouch, ready.

Concept 2Full draw

Archivo, black and extra wide

The band is pulled all the way back, below the word, about to let go.

Concept 3Pocket

Fredoka SemiBold, lowercase

A pocket slingshot: the y's descender becomes the handle.

Concept 4Landing dot

Sora ExtraBold

The stone has flown: it lands as the dot in the three front doors, slyng.app, slyng.money and slyng.ing.

Concept 5App icon

The slingshot on its own

The slingshot alone in a rounded square, made to be an app icon, and locked up with Loaded.

Picked: this icon

Concept 6Price sticker

Big Shoulders Display Black

A flea-market price sticker for the market, with a price tag loaded in the Y and Local goods and Digital goods around the edge.

Concept 7Monoline

Drawn from circles and lines

Every letter built from circles and straight lines of one weight, shown with its construction.

Concept 8Zap

Unbounded ExtraBold

A lightning bolt is loaded in the Y: sats travel as zaps. Ink and volt.

Picked: these colours

Next: concept 5, the app icon, in the colours of concept 8.

2

Round 2: The icon in Zap's colours

Concept 5's slingshot in white on ink with the band and stone in volt, and reversed in ink on volt. Lockups with Lexend and Unbounded; on ink, the bare slingshot stands in front of the word. And the first motion: pull back, let go, ring out, reload.

  1. Loaded
  2. Drawn back
  3. Released
  4. Empty
  5. Reloading

Next: make the icon the Y of the wordmark.

3

Round 3: The icon as the Y

The whole app icon set into the word as its Y, in Unbounded and in Lexend, with the pull and release playing inside it.

Next: not the whole icon, only the bare slingshot, the way it stood in front of the word on ink. The motion stays.

4

Round 4: The bare slingshot as the Y

The clean slingshot stands in for the Y. Without the icon's square around it, the stone leaves through the top of the frame and the next one drops back in the same way.

Next: a Y drawn in the same style as the other letters, the band lined up with its arms, and a dark animation without the shimmer.

5

Round 5: Unbounded's own Y

Two takes. Unbounded's own Y, untouched, with the band along the inside of its arms and the stone in the fork; and the rounded slingshot, drawn to the weight of the letters. The motion is now rendered as whole, opaque, lossless frames, which is what stops the shimmer on ink.

Next: the own Y on ink was the closest. The stone moves down onto the vertex, the bands run along the edges of the Y all the way to the tips, and the motion goes back to the earlier sequence.

6

Round 6: The logo

The stone sits on the vertex. The bands run up the inside of the arms, a hairline from the edge, and stop flush with the tops of the letters. The motion is the earlier pull and release again, and every loop ends in the logo pose.

Picked: this is the logo, the one at the top of this page.
