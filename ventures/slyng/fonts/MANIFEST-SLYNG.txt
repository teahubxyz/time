The fonts in this folder are derived works of the TeX Gyre fonts by the GUST e-foundry, distributed under the
GUST Font License (GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt in this folder; it refers to the LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c,
https://www.latex-project.org/lppl.txt).

As the license requests, the derived fonts and their files have new names:

  slyng-condensed-bold.woff  "Slyng Condensed Bold"   from TeX Gyre Heros Cn Bold   (texgyreheroscn-bold.otf)
  slyng-text-regular.woff    "Slyng Text Regular"     from TeX Gyre Adventor Regular (texgyreadventor-regular.otf)
  slyng-text-bold.woff       "Slyng Text Bold"        from TeX Gyre Adventor Bold    (texgyreadventor-bold.otf)

What changed: the glyph set was cut down to Latin-1 plus common punctuation, the OpenType features to kern, liga,
tnum, lnum and pnum, the name table to the names above, and the files were converted to WOFF. The outlines are
unchanged. Made for SLYNG (https://slyng.yolopreneur.lol/) in October 2026.
