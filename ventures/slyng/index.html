      SLNG

      The Y is the fork

      SLYNG started as a verb. I'll sling you some sats. I'll sling you a note later. It needed a spelling of its own, and the odd one turned out to be the point: the Y in the middle of the word is a slingshot.

      So the app is one. The people you follow stand on the fence. You aim by pulling the other way, the band tells you how much, and when you let go it lands with them: sats over lightning, and a note sealed so only they can read it.

      How it works

      • Aim

        Everyone you can sling to stands on the fence: the people you follow on nostr. Pull the stone away from the person you mean, the way a real slingshot works, and the dotted arc shows where it will go.

      • Stretch

        The further back you pull, the more you send. A short tug is a note on its own, then 21 sats, 100, 210, and on up to 21,000. Each ring is one step, so the amount is written out before you let go.

      • Let go

        The stone is in the air for a moment, and you can catch it back. When it lands, your wallet pays and your note goes, privately. On their side, it shows up as caught.

      Let an agent aim

      An agent can aim the slingshot. Only a person lets go.

      Any agent can make a link that opens SLYNG aimed at someone, with a note tied on and a suggested amount marked on the band. You read it, pull back, and let go, or you don't.

      https://slyng.yolopreneur.lol/#to=name@example.com&sats=2100&note=for%20the%20records

      Or hand your agent this prompt:

      Read https://slyng.yolopreneur.lol/skill.md, then make me a SLYNG link to send [who] [how many] sats with the note "[what to say]".

      An agent with a nostr key and a wallet of its own can sling by itself, the way SLYNG does: a zap for the sats and a gift-wrapped note for the words. The files: skill.md (the steps, for agents) and llms.txt (this list).

      The fine print

      No account, no server of ours
      This page talks to nostr relays and to the lightning address servers of the people you sling to, straight from your browser. Nothing goes through SLYNG.
      Your key
      With a nostr extension, your key never touches this page. A key made or pasted here stays in this browser, for this tab unless you ask to be remembered. It signs from a page, so keep it for everyday amounts and keep your main key in an extension.
      Your sats
      Sats go from your own wallet, through a browser wallet or Nostr Wallet Connect, straight to theirs. SLYNG never holds them. Before your wallet pays, the page checks that the invoice is for exactly the amount you pulled. Lightning payments are final.
      What's public
      Sats go as a zap, and zaps are public on nostr: anyone can see who sent how much to whom. The note travels separately, encrypted and gift-wrapped (NIP-17), so relays can see neither the words nor who sent them.
      Catch it back
      While the stone is in the air, you can catch it back and nothing is sent.
      Demo
      Until you sign in, the sats are pretend and the people on the fence are made up. Nothing leaves the page.
      Not affiliated
      SLYNG is independent, and isn't affiliated with any wallet, relay or app named here.
