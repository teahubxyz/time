How it works

1.

Type your slides

Markdown-ish text in the box: headings, bullets, paragraphs, --- between slides. The syntax is the stuff you already type.

2.

Watch it set live

Every keystroke re-typesets the deck on the right. Navigate with arrow keys or swipe; the progress bar keeps your place.

3.

Take the file with you

One button copies the deck as a single self-contained HTML file. Open it anywhere, present it offline, email it, done.

§5

Honest limits

  • Nothing is saved — close the tab and your text is gone. Copy the HTML or paste your source somewhere safe.
  • One layout, one typeface, no themes. The deck looks like this page, not like your brand.
  • No images, tables, or nested lists yet — headings, bullets, paragraphs, and inline emphasis only.
  • No speaker notes, no presenter view, no PDF export. That is the Pro idea, if enough people ask for it.
  • The copy button needs a modern browser with clipboard access; on very old browsers it may silently fail.
