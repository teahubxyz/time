How it works

  1. Type the name of any everyday thing and pick a rarity. The same name always rolls the same affixes.
  2. Optionally add a photo (pixelated in your browser into 16×16 item art), your own lines, or your own flavor text.
  3. Download a transparent PNG, or copy a link: it holds the whole card, and opening it drops the item with a beam of light and a clink.

Honest limits

  • The word lists are small, so affixes and flavor lines repeat across different items.
  • A link carries a 16×16 version of your photo, not the original, so links with a photo are long.
  • Nothing is saved: no accounts, no collection. Your item exists only in the PNG or the link you keep.
  • The clink may stay silent until you have clicked or tapped the page, because browsers block early audio.
  • A fan-made style homage. It uses no game assets and is not affiliated with any game or publisher.
