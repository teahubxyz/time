## What is Hearthframe?

Hearthframe is a free, browser-based tool that transforms any device into a beautiful digital photo frame. Simply drop in photos from your device, choose a frame style, add a caption, and enjoy a full-window slideshow with a clock display.

Everything works entirely in your browser - no photos are uploaded to any server, no accounts are required, and no subscription is needed.

## Who is it for?

Hearthframe is designed for adult children setting up family photos for a parent. It's perfect for anyone who wants to repurpose an old tablet or phone as a dedicated photo frame without the hassle of dealing with consumer photo frames that demand apps, cloud accounts, and ongoing tech support.

## How to use it

1. Open [hearthframe.yolopreneur.lol](https://hearthframe.yolopreneur.lol/) on any device
2. Drag photos from your device or click to select files
3. Choose a frame mat, clock style, and transition effect
4. Add a caption for your slideshow
5. Click "Preview" and use your browser's full-screen mode
6. Adjust your device's settings to prevent sleep mode if needed

For continuous operation on a tablet:
- Enable "Stay Awake" in developer options to prevent the screen from turning off
- Adjust screen timeout settings to "Never" or the longest duration available

## Features

- **No uploads**: All processing happens locally in your browser
- **No account required**: Use it immediately without signing up
- **No subscription**: Completely free to use
- **Self-contained HTML file**: Download your frame to reopen later
- **Multiple frame styles**: Choose from classic wood, modern black, vintage tan, or minimalist white
- **Customizable clock**: Digital time or date & time display
- **Transition effects**: Fade, slide, or instant transitions
- **Personal captions**: Add meaningful text to your slideshow

## Honest limits

Hearthframe is intentionally simple and focused:

- No automatic photo syncing - you need to manually update the frame
- No cloud storage - all photos must be stored locally on the device
- No multi-user collaboration - only one person can manage the frame
- No advanced scheduling - photos rotate at fixed intervals
- No mobile app - only browser-based solution currently available
