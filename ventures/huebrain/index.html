Huebrain needs JavaScript to train its net in this page. Nothing is uploaded either way.

How it works

  1. Tap Love or Nope on 20 color swatches, one at a time.
  2. A small neural net retrains in the page after every tap, and its neurons glow as they fire.
  3. See a heat map of the color wheel, a 5-color palette and guesses on new swatches. Download a wallpaper PNG or copy a link; a partner who opens it and taps their own 20 sees both brains side by side with a match score.

Honest limits

  • The net is tiny (4 inputs, 8 neurons) and learns from only 20 taps, so its taste map is a rough guess, not a measurement.
  • Colors are shown on your screen. Real paint looks different in real light, and the page does not match to paint brands.
  • Nothing is saved. Reload and your brain is gone unless you copy the link or download the wallpaper.
  • The share link holds the net's 49 weights (not your taps), and anyone with it can see your taste map. It compares two brains only: theirs and yours.
  • The match score is the average agreement of the two nets' yes-scores over a grid of colors. It is a rough number, not a verdict.
