Honest limits

  • The PNG is screen resolution (1280×880 for the pair, 600×840 for one side), RGB. It is not 300 dpi or CMYK, and there is no box template.
  • Cropping is a centered zoom with two pan sliders. No face detection, no rotation.
  • The three styles are original looks inspired by their eras. They are not copies of real boxes or logos, and the rating badges are not official ratings.
  • The fake screenshots are pixelated crops of your photo, not game footage.
  • Nothing is saved. Reload the page and you start over. Text uses your device's fonts, so it can look slightly different from one device to another.
