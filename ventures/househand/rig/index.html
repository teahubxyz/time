house hand

Pose and animate

Drag the pink handles to move the hands, feet and head, and the ring at the hips to move the body. Save two poses as A and B, then play: the blend is one SVG that animates itself. Everything is drawn by house.py, running in your browser. Watch Bunny and Unicorn say hello, a longer piece animated with the same rig.

Loading Python in your browser. About 10 MB, the first time only.

Character
Poses
Hands

Left and right as you see them. In profile only the near arm shows.

Body

Drag the ring at the hips to move the body while the feet stay planted: down to crouch, up to stretch, sideways to shift the weight. Hold Shift to move the whole character, feet and all.

Layers

    Front to back. Move an arm past the body to bring it in front or put it behind, or below the line to hide it. Auto lets the view decide: in ¾ the arm and leg nearer you are on top.

    Face
    Ears
    Ears and tail

    The tail and the long mane show in ¾, profile and back views.

    Blend

    Everything you drag or slide morphs from A to B. What can't morph, like another view or face, switches halfway.
